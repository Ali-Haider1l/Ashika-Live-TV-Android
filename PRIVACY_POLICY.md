# Ashika Live TV — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026  
**App:** Ashika Live TV for Android  
**Package:** `com.ashika.livetv.android`

This is a privacy-policy draft for the Android app. The publisher must review it against the final app build, advertising account settings, and the laws that apply before public release.

## What the app does

Ashika Live TV is a player for playlists and stream URLs that the user chooses to add. The app does not provide or own television channels. Users are responsible for having permission to access the playlists and streams they add.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores playlist entries, imported playlist content, favourites, recently watched channels, and app settings locally on your device using browser storage. These items are used to provide app features. The app does not require you to create an account.

When you choose a remote playlist, programme guide, logo, or video stream, your device connects to the relevant third-party server. Those providers may receive your IP address and request information under their own privacy policies.

## Advertising and consent

The Android edition is designed to use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The current development build uses Google's sample test app and banner-ad identifiers only; test ads do not generate publisher revenue.

If the publisher enables live advertising, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising events to deliver, measure, and protect ads. The app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow where required. Available choices depend on your location and Google's consent requirements.

The publisher must replace sample test identifiers with its own AdMob identifiers and configure the correct consent messages before enabling live ads. Do not click test ads except as part of legitimate development testing.

## Third-party services

The app may use:
- Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent in Android builds.
- The Android System WebView for displaying the app interface and supported web media.
- HLS.js and dash.js libraries loaded from their respective CDN when the app runs, for compatible streaming formats.
- Playlist, EPG, channel-logo, and streaming providers selected by the user.

Those services have their own terms and privacy policies. The publisher should link to the current Google privacy disclosures and any other relevant provider disclosures in the final store listing.

## Children's privacy

The publisher has not configured this development build as a child-directed app. Before release, the publisher must select the correct audience and age settings in Google Play Console and AdMob. Do not target children or serve personalized ads to children. If the app is intended for children or mixed audiences, additional Google Families requirements and legal obligations apply.

## Data security and retention

Locally stored playlist data remains on the device until the user clears app data, removes the app, or deletes the playlist through the app. The publisher does not operate an account backend for this app in the current implementation. Third-party providers may retain their own request and advertising data under their policies.

## Your choices

You can remove saved playlists from the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Where a consent form is presented, use it to review the available advertising choices. Consent and privacy choices may also be available through Google's privacy and ad settings.
