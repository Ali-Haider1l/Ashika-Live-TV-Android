ASHIKA LIVE TV · ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This is a development policy draft. The publisher must review and complete it before public release.

What the app does

Ashika Live TV is a player for playlists and stream URLs that users choose to add. It does not provide or own television channels. Users must have permission to access the playlists and streams they add.

Information stored on your device

Playlist entries, imported playlist content, favourites, recently watched channels and app settings are stored locally on your device using browser storage. The current implementation does not require an account.

When you choose a remote playlist, programme guide, logo or video stream, your device connects to that third-party server. Providers may receive your IP address and request information under their own policies.

Advertising and consent

The Android edition is integrated with Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). This development build uses Google's sample test app and banner-ad identifiers only; test ads do not generate publisher revenue.

If live ads are enabled in a future release, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and advertising events to deliver, measure and protect ads. Consent choices depend on location and applicable requirements.

Third-party services

The app may use Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP, Android System WebView, HLS.js, dash.js, and playlist, EPG, logo and streaming providers selected by the user. Each provider has its own terms and privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The publisher must set the correct audience and age settings in Google Play Console and AdMob before release. This development build has not been configured as a child-directed app. If the app is intended for children or mixed audiences, additional requirements apply.

Data retention and choices

Locally stored data remains until users remove it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Users can remove playlists in the app and review available ad-consent choices when a consent form is presented. Third-party providers may retain their own data under their policies.

Contact

Publisher contact: The publisher must add a monitored privacy-support email address before public release.

Policy updates

This policy must be updated if data practices, advertising partners, analytics, permissions or backend services change. The publisher is responsible for ensuring the published policy accurately describes the released app and complies with applicable law.
